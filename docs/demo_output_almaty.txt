    Route finding is a search over the street graph; an admissible, consistent heuristic lets A*
    return the same optimal route as Dijkstra while expanding far fewer nodes.

ASSUMPTIONS
==============================================================================
  - PM2.5 values are model readings from the CAMS global model (~40 km grid) served by Open-Meteo, not sensor measurements; past hours are earlier model runs.
  - Class cut-points 5 / 15 / 25 ug/m3 come from WHO 2021 daily and annual guidance and are applied to hourly values; WHO defines no hourly PM2.5 guideline.
  - Reading trust r (default 0.8) is a setting, not estimated from data: the probability that the grid value's band is the true class on your street.
  - Forecast trust r_f(k) = max(0.5, 0.75 - 0.005 k) for an hour k hours ahead.
  - Transitions are learned from model readings: the reading's band stands in for the hidden class.
  - Harm per hour [0, 1, 4, 10], activity benefits and the waiting cost are utility points chosen by hand.
  - The action now uses the QMDP rule: it assumes the uncertainty about the class disappears after one step.
  - The traffic exposure index per street kind and the 25 m roadside rule are a proxy for traffic pollution next to busy roads, not measured values.
  - Ventilation 1.2 m3/h walking and 3.0 m3/h running (US EPA Exposure Factors Handbook 2011, ch. 6); walking 80 m/min, running 160 m/min.
  - Not medical advice.
